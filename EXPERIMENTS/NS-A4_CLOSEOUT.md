# NS-A4 — Flux ↔ Stretching Coupling Closeout

**Status:** CLOSED / NOT_PROVEN  
**Date:** 2026-09-10

## Result

The proposed bridge between dyadic kinetic-energy flux and vorticity stretching was formulated and self-attacked.

The structural identities do not by themselves provide the missing bridge. Total kinetic-energy transfer is conservative across shells, while enstrophy production depends on one additional derivative and can concentrate at high frequency. Consequently, energy flux conservation cannot be promoted into enstrophy control without a new coercive estimate.

The stronger proposed statement — that every dangerous positive stretching event must be accompanied by sufficiently favorable outward high-frequency flux — is also not established. Triadic interactions permit signed transfers, including high-high-to-low interactions, so the sign relation cannot be assumed.

## What survives

- Exact energy-shell transfer identity.
- Exact enstrophy production identity.
- Geometric interpretation of stretching through `ω·Sω`.
- Clear formulation of the missing bridge: a scale-uniform, critical, signed interaction estimate that couples stretching to transfer and dissipation.

## What fails as a proof shortcut

- finite energy ⇒ enstrophy control;
- positive stretching ⇒ necessarily positive outward flux;
- shellwise sign assumptions;
- numerical correlation ⇒ universal theorem;
- conditional flux control ⇒ proof of the condition.

## Classification

**NOT_PROVEN.**

No solution, singularity construction, or global regularity theorem was obtained.

## Handoff

The A2/A3/A4 sequence has now isolated the common bottleneck: a genuinely coercive critical estimate capable of controlling the nonlinear derivative concentration. Future work should only open a new branch if it introduces a mathematically new mechanism rather than another reformulation of the same energy/enstrophy identities.
